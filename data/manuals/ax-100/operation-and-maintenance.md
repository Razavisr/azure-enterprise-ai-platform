## Equipment purpose

The AX-100 is a fictional compact auxiliary pump. Its operating limits are different from those of the PX-200, so technicians must use the correct equipment manual.

## Normal operating range

Normal casing temperature is between 40°C and 70°C. Vibration should remain at or below 3.0 mm/s. Discharge pressure should remain between 2.0 and 3.0 bar, and flow should remain between 80 and 120 litres per minute.

## Abnormal vibration

Vibration above 5.5 mm/s is a high-severity condition for the AX-100. Inspect the mounting bracket, flexible coupling, and bearing assembly.

Do not apply PX-200 operating thresholds or maintenance procedures to the AX-100.

## High temperature

Temperature above 82°C is a high-severity condition. Stop the unit using the approved AX-100 shutdown procedure and arrange an inspection of the cooling path and bearing assembly.
