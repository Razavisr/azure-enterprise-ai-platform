# PX-200 Operation and Maintenance Manual

> Synthetic documentation created exclusively for the Azure Enterprise AI Platform demonstration. Do not use it to operate real equipment.

## Equipment purpose

The PX-200 is a fictional centrifugal process pump designed for continuous water circulation. Operators should monitor casing temperature, vibration, discharge pressure, flow rate, motor current, and hours since maintenance.

## Normal operating range

During stable operation, casing temperature should remain between 45°C and 80°C. Vibration should remain at or below 4.5 mm/s. Discharge pressure should be between 4.5 and 6.0 bar, flow rate between 150 and 220 litres per minute, and motor current between 8 and 14 amperes.

Readings outside these ranges require investigation, especially when multiple readings become abnormal simultaneously.

## Abnormal vibration

Vibration between 4.5 and 7.1 mm/s is a warning condition. Inspect mounting bolts, shaft alignment, bearing lubrication, and suction conditions.

Vibration above 7.1 mm/s is a high-severity condition that can indicate bearing wear, shaft misalignment, imbalance, or cavitation. If high vibration occurs together with casing temperature above 90°C or a sudden pressure decrease, initiate a controlled shutdown and request maintenance inspection.

## High temperature

A casing temperature between 80°C and 90°C is a warning condition. Check lubrication, cooling flow, operating load, and bearing condition.

Temperature above 90°C is a high-severity condition. Temperature at or above 105°C requires immediate shutdown according to the approved site procedure. Do not restart the pump until an authorized technician identifies and corrects the cause.

## Low pressure or flow

Discharge pressure below 3.5 bar or flow below 120 litres per minute is a high-severity condition. Possible causes include a blocked suction line, a partially closed valve, fluid leakage, cavitation, or impeller damage.

Inspect the suction path, valve position, visible leakage, and inlet conditions before returning the pump to service.

## High motor current

Motor current between 14 and 17 amperes is a warning condition. Current above 17 amperes is a high-severity condition and can indicate mechanical obstruction, excessive load, bearing friction, or an electrical fault.

Do not repeatedly reset an overloaded motor without identifying the underlying cause.

## Maintenance prioritization

A predictive failure score can help prioritize inspections, but it does not authorize an automatic shutdown or maintenance action. High-risk recommendations must be reviewed by a qualified operator or maintenance engineer.

Before physical inspection, isolate energy sources and follow the approved lockout and tagout procedure.
